About

I'm a civil engineer and Engineer Intern (E.I.) at Civil & Environmental Consultants in Columbus, Ohio. I prepare civil site designs for commercial, industrial and residential land development, including grading, utilities, erosion and sediment control, water quality BMPs and SWPPPs, and I support permitting with regulatory agencies.

Before CEC, I worked on facility specifications, constructability reviews and bid documents with FedEx's Design Engineering Services team, and I designed and inspected highway projects with the National Highway Authority.

That mix of design, construction and owner-side experience lets me see a project the way the designer, the contractor and the owner each see it. I'm working toward my PE license.
